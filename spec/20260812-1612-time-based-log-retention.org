#+title: Time-based log retention
#+date: 2026-08-12 16:12 UTC
#+license: GFDL-1.3-or-later

* Status

Implemented and locally validated on 2026-08-12 after maintainer approval.

* Goal

Let operators bound how long records remain replayable without turning
retention into periodic whole-store deletion.

* Configuration

#+begin_src yaml
store:
  max_memory: 128MiB
  max_logs: 500
  retention: 1h
#+end_src

=retention= is required to be a positive integer followed immediately by one
unit: =s= for seconds, =m= for minutes, or =h= for hours. Examples are =30s=,
=15m=, and =2h=. Fractions, compound durations, whitespace, zero, missing
units, uppercase units, overflow, and unknown units fail startup.

The default is =1h= so an omitted store section remains useful and bounded.

* Semantics

Each accepted record receives a userspace monotonic insertion instant. It
expires when its age reaches =retention=. Kernel event timestamps are not used:
their clock domain is a capture fact, while retention begins when the store
accepts ownership.

Expiry evicts individual records in store insertion order. It never clears a
young record merely because an older record expired. Empty workload rings and
Pod counters are cleaned through the same eviction path as count and memory
pressure. Existing replay/live references remain valid shared references after
the record leaves retained history; expiry cannot revoke data already handed
to a subscriber.

The store actor owns a Tokio interval and expires records without incoming
commands. Append and subscribe also expire overdue records before operating, so
timer scheduling delay cannot expose stale history at those boundaries. The
timer cadence is at most one second and never exceeds the configured duration.

Retention eviction has its own counter. It is distinct from per-workload count
and global memory-pressure eviction.

* Acceptance

- =Ns=, =Nm=, and =Nh= parse to the exact duration and round-trip through the
  store configuration.
- Invalid and overflowing spellings fail configuration validation.
- Records remain replayable before their age reaches retention and disappear
  at the boundary.
- Mixed-age records expire individually; workload and Pod accounting stays
  exact as rings become empty.
- Expiry runs while the actor is idle and before subscription replay.
- Existing replay references survive expiry.
- Retention, README, manifest, field guide, and roadmap describe the same
  behavior.
