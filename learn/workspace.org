#+title: The BLT crate map
#+license: GFDL-1.3-or-later

BLT is three programs and three Cargo packages. The collector is the trusted
cluster ingress for logs and pushed metrics.

#+begin_example
boring-logtail -- OTLP Logs/gRPC --------> boring-collector --> Loki
boring-probe --- OTLP Metrics/gRPC ------> boring-collector --> Prometheus
instrumented apps -- OTLP Metrics/gRPC -> boring-collector --> Prometheus
#+end_example

* Node agent modules

The =model/= module owns captured records and Kubernetes identity. The =otlp/=
module maps those facts to OTLP Logs and owns bounded delivery and retry.

=boring-probe= maps kubelet Summary snapshots into OTLP Metrics and sends them
through the same collector service used by instrumented applications.

* Collector modules

The =ports/= module owns complete OTLP writes and typed log reads. The =stores/=
module contains the Loki and Prometheus production adapters plus the small
in-memory log adapter for tests and local experiments.

* The binaries

=boring-logtail= owns CRI-file lifecycle, partial joining, Kubernetes watches,
and bounded collector delivery. =boring-probe= owns kubelet Summary collection.
=boring-collector= owns stateless OTLP ingress, bulk Loki and Prometheus
delivery, health, and the trusted gRPC reader adapter.

Put code with the facts or resource lifecycle it owns. Leave a moving boundary
in its composition root until evidence earns another crate.
